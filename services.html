Embroidery
Dept. III — Fine Needlework

Custom hand and machine embroidery is worked upon elegant fabrics, drawing inspiration from antique textiles and vintage embroidery designs of a bygone era.

Every piece is handcrafted and made in America, whether a monogram on a handkerchief or a full embellished panel for a wedding gown.
