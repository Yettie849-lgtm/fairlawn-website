The Proprietress

Once upon a time, garments of beauty were made entirely by hand — every stroke of the stitch a labor of love. Antique textiles and vintage embroidery inspire our proprietress to take something old and sew it anew.

Self-taught from the basics her mother gave her, she was gifted her first Singer sewing machine at the tender age of twelve, and has scarcely set it down since.
