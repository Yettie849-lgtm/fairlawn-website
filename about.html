Her Story

The Proprietress

Self-Taught, Since Age Twelve

Once upon a time, garments of beauty were made entirely by hand — every stroke of the stitch a labor of love. Antique textiles and vintage embroidery designs inspire our proprietress to sew her own updated version of something old, and make it new again.

As a self-taught seamstress, she was given the basics of sewing by her mother, and by the age of twelve she had been gifted her first Singer sewing machine. She scarcely put it down since.

Every design that leaves Fairlawn is handcrafted and made in America. She takes great pride in her work, in hopes that it will be appreciated for many years to come — not just worn for a season.

— As told by the House of Fairlawn
